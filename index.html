Franz Liszt

Franz Liszt (22 October 1811 – 31 July 1886) was a Hungarian composer, virtuoso pianist, conductor and teacher of the Romantic period. With a diverse body of work spanning more than six decades, he is considered to be one of the most prolific and influential composers of his era, and his piano works continue to be widely performed and recorded.

Liszt achieved success as a concert pianist from an early age, and received lessons from the esteemed musicians Carl Czerny and Antonio Salieri. He gained further renown for his performances during tours of Europe in the 1830s and 1840s, developing a reputation for technical brilliance as well as physical attractiveness. In a phenomenon dubbed "Lisztomania", he rose to a degree of stardom and popularity among the public not experienced by the virtuosos who had preceded him.

This website is a heartfelt dedication to his virtuosity, his artistry, and his lasting influence on music. Liszt pushed the boundaries of what was thought possible on the piano, transforming the instrument into something almost orchestral. His performances captivated audiences across Europe and helped shape the image of the modern virtuoso. Beyond his technical brilliance, his music carried an extraordinary emotional depth and ambition. This site celebrates both the legendary pianist and the composer behind the music. It is a small tribute to a musician whose influence continues to resonate today.
